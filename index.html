Services

Servo services we deliver

From single axis retrofit to multi-axis packaging lines.

Sizing & Selection

Torque, inertia ratio, encoder resolution and ambient conditions matched to the right motor–drive pair.

Auto-Tuning & Gains

Position/speed/torque loops, notch filters and damping so axes settle cleanly without vibration.

Install & Commission

Wiring, encoder cables, STO safety, homing routines and full load verification on site.

Multi-Axis Sync

Electronic gearing, camming and coordinated motion with PLC or dedicated motion controllers.

Retrofit & Upgrade

Replace aging servos with modern drives while keeping mechanics and minimizing downtime.

Repair & Diagnostics

Alarm analysis, encoder/drive repair, parameter recovery and preventive health checks.

Process

How a servo project runs

Clear steps from load calculation to stable production.

STEP 01

Load & inertia study

Mass, friction, duty cycle and required accuracy — motor and drive selected to match.

STEP 02

Wire, power & safety

Power, encoder, STO and network cables installed to OEM and EMC best practice.

STEP 03

Tune & integrate

Auto-tune, filters, homing and PLC/motion link — verified under real load.

STEP 04

Document & support

Parameter backup, operator notes and ongoing fault response when you need it.
