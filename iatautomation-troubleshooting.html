What We Troubleshoot

Complete Industrial Automation Support

From fault codes to complete system recovery — we handle the critical equipment that keeps your production running.

VFD Troubleshooting

Overcurrent, overvoltage, ground fault, overheating, communication loss, parameter corruption and power stage failures.

  • Fault code diagnosis & reset
  • Parameter backup & restore
  • Power module & capacitor repair
  • Cooling fan & IGBT service

PLC Troubleshooting

Program errors, I/O module failures, communication drops, battery faults, scan time issues and unexpected stops.

  • Logic & program analysis
  • I/O module testing & replace
  • Network & bus diagnostics
  • Online monitoring & force

HMI Troubleshooting

Blank screens, touch failure, communication loss with PLC, tag errors, project corruption and firmware issues.

  • Screen & touch calibration
  • Project backup & download
  • Driver & protocol fix
  • Panel replacement support

Servo Drive Troubleshooting

Position errors, encoder faults, overcurrent, tuning instability, following error and mechanical load issues.

  • Encoder & feedback check
  • Auto-tuning & gain adjust
  • Wiring & shielding review
  • Drive & motor matching
